About us

Grown up alongside Korea's steel industry

Founded in 2002 in Pohang, the heart of Korea's steel industry, THREEK has spent more than two decades supplying carbon raw materials to electric arc furnace (EAF) steelmakers, lime calcination plants and foundries. We source anthracite, coke and graphite from overseas suppliers, then dry, cool, screen, crush, blend and pack them in our own plant so that every shipment matches the customer's required particle size, moisture, grade and packing type.

Recarburizer

02 · EAF SLAG

Slag Foaming Agent

Fine, fully dried carbon powder injected into the EAF to foam the slag, improving heat efficiency and protecting the furnace lining.

Size

Moisture
Fully dried
Users
